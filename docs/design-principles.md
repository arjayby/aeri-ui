# Aeri UI Design Principles

## Calm frame, expressive interaction

The Catalog shell is quiet and editorial so Registry Item Live Previews carry the visual energy. Layouts use generous space, neutral surfaces, and one restrained cool accent rather than competing gradients, glass, glow, or rainbow borders.

## Interaction-led Motion

Surfaces remain still by default. Motion acknowledges interaction or communicates state, and a viewport may contain at most one restrained ambient accent. Motion must never delay input, obscure state, or compete for attention.

## Distinct from the reference

Aeri UI takes inspiration from the interaction quality of beUI without reproducing its dark-neon presentation. Motion belongs primarily in Live Preview behavior rather than in a spectacle-driven hero or animated Catalog chrome.

## Theme parity

Light and dark modes are equally supported, and the Catalog follows the operating-system preference by default. Registry Items inherit the Consumer Theme rather than carrying the Catalog's brand treatment into installed source.

## Performance is part of the aesthetic

An effect that misses the Performance Contract is not visually acceptable. Restraint, responsiveness, and clear state communication take precedence over novelty.
